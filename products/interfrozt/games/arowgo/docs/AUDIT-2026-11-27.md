## Scope

New worlds use forty levels; Clockwork has four ten-stop map pages, 120 additional stars and 1,000 first-clear coins. Total campaign: 140 levels, 420 stars, 3,500 first-clear coins.

## Level findings

All forty validate, have distinct geometry, and have a complete legal solution. All have 28 arrows, at least 89 occupied cells, and witnessed legal choices with both winning and losing continuations. Pulse gates use a three-removal cycle; window gates use inclusive removal-count intervals. Attachments do not overlap key/anchor sources. Each ten-level block is sorted by structural rating. Scores may fall at block boundaries while a new mechanic is introduced; this is not a strictly monotonic 40-level score sequence.

The version-one score uses the existing frozen calibration. Its probability reflects all mechanics; its timing component still measures keg slack only. Failure horizon comes from one witnessed losing choice, not a proof of human lookahead. Random-legal-play probability is not a human success rate or IQ score.
