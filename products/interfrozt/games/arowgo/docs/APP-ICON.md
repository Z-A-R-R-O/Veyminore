## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished square mobile app icon for AROWGO, a polished casual snake-arrow puzzle game. Create ONE bold recognizable icon, straight-on, full bleed square opaque artwork. Central symbol is one continuous chunky golden arrow with a softly rounded zigzag path: short tail rising from lower left, bending right across the middle then upward, ending in a large clear upward-pointing arrowhead at upper right. Smooth 90-degree elbow bends, readable silhouette, absolutely no branching, no extra arrowheads. Luxurious candy-like sculpted gold with warm amber shaded edges and restrained creamy highlights; charming premium game art, not metallic realism. Deep rich violet to bright amethyst background with a soft central glow, subtle rounded inset bevel giving depth. Symbol occupies central 68 percent, ample safe margin for launcher masking. Strong small-size contrast. No lettering, no flowers, no snake face, no eyes, no crown, no coins, no gems, no scenery, no border outside the square, no mockup, no watermarks. Clean expertly art-directed restrained glossy 3D mobile puzzle identity.
