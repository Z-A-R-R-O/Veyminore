## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design a radically different, memorable minimalist app logo for AROWGO, a snake-arrow logic puzzle game. ONE finished square app icon. Aim for the simplicity and instant recognition of a great enduring technology brand, but create an entirely original symbol; do not use or imitate an apple silhouette or any existing company logo. Symbol concept: a clever bold geometric lowercase-style abstract capital A monogram constructed from ONE broad continuous rounded ribbon path, with a single small integrated arrow tip rising at the upper-right end. A clean triangular negative-space counter makes the A recognizable, while the bent path conveys a puzzle route. Prioritize an ingenious unified silhouette over literal detail. Flat near-white mark on an immaculate saturated periwinkle-blue full-bleed square background. Subtle optical curvature and carefully balanced proportions. The mark occupies approximately 58 percent of the square, centered with generous breathing room. Swiss-quality identity design, premium and playful through geometry alone, sharp vector-like edges. No typography or spelled words, no gradients, no 3D, no gold, no bevels, no shadows, no glow, no texture, no border, no sparkles, no small decorative pieces, no multiple logos, no presentation sheet, no device mockup. Single isolated logo icon only. The result should be unmistakable at 24 pixels and beautiful as a monochrome silhouette.
