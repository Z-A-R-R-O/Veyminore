## 2. Codebase inspection & bug fixes delivered

During the full-codebase inspection, several subtle UI, accessibility, asset routing, and synchronization bugs were identified and fixed:

### Fix 1: Game board accessibility announcement for late-game worlds
* **Location**: `src/ui/App.tsx`
* **Issue**: The board card's `aria-label` contained a ternary that only checked `levelNumber > 40 ? "Frostfall Peaks" : levelNumber > 20 ? "Verdant Cove" : "Bloom Valley"`. As a result, screen readers misannounced Levels 61–100 (Amber Dunes and Amber Sanctum) as Frostfall Peaks levels.
* **Resolution**: Updated the ternary to evaluate `levelNumber > 80 ? "Amber Sanctum" : levelNumber > 60 ? "Amber Dunes" : levelNumber > 40 ? "Frostfall Peaks" : levelNumber > 20 ? "Verdant Cove" : "Bloom Valley"`.

### Fix 2: World completion eyebrow banner in Finale
* **Location**: `src/ui/Finale.tsx`
* **Issue**: `Finale.tsx` hardcoded `{world === "bloom" ? "WORLD 01 · COMPLETE" : "ALL WORLDS · COMPLETE"}`. Clearing intermediate chapters (Cove, Frost, Amber) incorrectly displayed the final campaign victory header.
* **Resolution**: Replaced with a complete five-world mapping (`world === "bloom" ? "WORLD 01" : world === "cove" ? "WORLD 02" : world === "frost" ? "WORLD 03" : world === "amber" ? "WORLD 04" : "ALL WORLDS · COMPLETE"`).

### Fix 3: Root-relative asset path resolution in Scenery
* **Location**: `src/ui/Scenery.tsx`
* **Issue**: Bloom Valley scenery used relative `./assets/bloom-valley-*.png` while Cove, Frost, and Amber used `/assets/...`. When accessing URLs with query parameters or subpaths (e.g., `/?account=1#profile`), relative paths could fail to resolve.
* **Resolution**: Normalized all picture source URLs to root-relative `/assets/...`.

### Fix 4: Web Audio resilience and SSR / test safety
* **Location**: `src/ui/Sound.tsx`
* **Issue**: Audio clip instantiation (`new Audio(...)`) was executed eagerly at the module root, risking unhandled exceptions in environments where `Audio` is missing or throws. In addition, `AudioContext` did not fall back to prefixed `webkitAudioContext`.
* **Resolution**: Encapsulated clip creation with `typeof Audio !== "undefined"` checks, added graceful error catching, and added `webkitAudioContext` compatibility fallback.

### Fix 5: Timing guide label for Amber Sanctum
* **Location**: `src/ui/TimingGuide.tsx`
* **Issue**: Timing rules button `aria-label` only checked `level.levelId.startsWith("amber-")`, causing Sanctum levels (`sanctum-81`..`100`) to default to "Frostfall rules".
* **Resolution**: Added explicit check for `level.levelId.startsWith("sanctum-")` returning "Sanctum timing rules".

### Fix 6: Live appearance synchronization to server ledger
* **Location**: `src/account/economy-runtime.ts` & `src/ui/Prototype.tsx`
* **Issue**: While the server ledger schema supported `avatar`, `frame`, and `name` in `preferences`, the client-side runtime did not expose `updateAppearance()` or propagate changes from `saveProfile()` in `Prototype.tsx` to the backend when an account was bound.
* **Resolution**: Added `updateAppearance()` in `economy-runtime.ts` with optimistic revision locking, integrated it into `saveProfile()`, and updated `accountProfile()` to hydrate the user's remote appearance into the active profile.
