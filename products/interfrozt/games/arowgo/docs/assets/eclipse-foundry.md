Prompts requested polished whimsical 3D mobile-game art, emerald/jade crystals, dark volcanic rock, warm gold machinery, an eclipse, no lettering or interface, and distinct portrait/landscape compositions. The home prompt requested a fully visible centered island on transparency. Maps were converted to WebP at quality 88; the original generated outputs were retained outside the repository. Ten map controls use scene-specific positions; paired controls share broad portrait terraces.

Switch/gate badges are resolution-independent SVG rendered by Board.tsx, not raster art: numbered jade/amber symbols, diamond switches, square gates, and different open/closed bars. Their diameter is bounded by the board cell size. Accessible descriptions carry the same meaning without relying on colour.
